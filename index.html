Microphone access is optional. It listens locally for the low "thud" of the ball hitting the rebounder (60 to 250 Hz) and counts one shot per sharp thud, with a short cooldown so net wobble is not double counted. Nothing is recorded or uploaded.

Set the phone on a stand behind the rebounder with the ball and the player's whole body in view. Good light helps a lot.
